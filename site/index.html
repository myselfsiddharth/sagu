A tiny cactus for your group chat.

Sagu lives in your iMessage groups. It plays games, shares food deals and gets everyone to say "in", and now and then reminds a friend what they said. Only the friends who asked for it.

What Sagu does

Small, quick, and a little bit pushy.

🧾

Receipts

Say "sagu roast me" and Sagu may quote something you said in that chat, like a gym plan that never happened. Never anyone who didn't opt in.

🌮

Food deals

Share a deal, Sagu counts the "in" replies, proposes a time, and reminds everyone an hour before.

🎲

Games

Trivia, number guessing, hangman, would-you-rather, 8-ball and more, with a scoreboard.

🤫

Quiet by default

Silent and storing nothing in a chat until the host texts "sagu on". Quiet hours and a daily cap after that.

How it works

No server, no account, no app. Just your Mac.

  1. Reads your Messages database on your Mac (read-only), only for chats where you said "sagu on".
  2. Thinks locally: picks a receipt, counts "in" replies, runs the game, checks every line against its hard-line filter.
  3. Sends through the Messages app using your own Apple ID. Every line starts with 🌵.

Privacy and hard lines

Built so it is hard to be creepy.

Opt-in only

Only people who text "sagu roast me" are ever quoted. "sagu spare me" ends it and deletes what Sagu saved. "sagu forget me" deletes everything.

Stays on your Mac

Data lives in ~/.config/sagu. Nothing is sent to any service unless you turn on the optional AI lines yourself.

Roast the excuse, not the person

A filter blocks jokes about bodies, weight, looks, race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, mental health, family or money. No slurs, threats or sexual content.
